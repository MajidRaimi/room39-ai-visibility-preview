# How to Get Your Med Spa to Show Up in ChatGPT

ChatGPT names your med spa when three conditions line up: its search layer can find your pages, it can lift a clean answer from them, and it can find the same facts about you on other trusted sites. There is no ad to buy and no switch to flip. You earn the mention by being crawlable, extractable, and confirmed elsewhere.

This guide shows the six things that decide it, then gives you a 30-day plan. It is written for a one-to-three location clinic owner, not for an enterprise marketing team.

## What "showing up in ChatGPT" actually means

When someone asks ChatGPT for "the best med spa for Botox in Scottsdale," the answer can come from two places:

1. **Live web search.** When search is on, ChatGPT retrieves pages and can cite them as sources. To be cited, your pages must be reachable and clear.
2. **Training data.** Older knowledge baked into the model. You cannot change this directly, but consistent public facts about your clinic improve the odds your brand is at least recognised.

The practical takeaway: optimize for the live-retrieval path. It is the part you control. Google makes the same point in its [guide to optimizing for generative AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): there is no special markup required, and helpful, people-first content still wins.

This matters more each year. A 2025 analysis by [Andreessen Horowitz](https://a16z.com/geo-over-seo/) found that AI-native search queries average about 23 words, versus roughly 4 for traditional search, and sessions run around 6 minutes. People describe their situation in full sentences. Long, specific questions need long, specific answers.

## Why this is a med spa problem specifically

The US has roughly [10,488 medical spas as of 2023](https://www.aesthetichires.com/med-spa-industry-statistics/), and the industry passes [$17 billion in revenue](https://americanmedspa.org/med-spa-statistics/). Many clinics in that pool are competing for the same local searches with near-identical websites.

The economics make the gap expensive. Aesthetic patient lifetime value commonly runs [$3,000 to $8,000](https://skinspire.agency/medspa-digital-marketing/med-spa-seo-services/) according to agency-published figures. Losing one new patient a month to a competitor who gets named in the AI answer is a five-figure annual swing.

## The six things that decide whether ChatGPT cites you

### 1. Can AI crawlers reach your site?

If your `robots.txt` blocks `GPTBot` or `ChatGPT-User`, OpenAI cannot fetch your pages for search. Check yours now. Many clinics inherited a block from a previous agency or a security plugin and never knew.

- OpenAI's crawler documentation: [platform.openai.com/docs/bots](https://platform.openai.com/docs/bots)
- For Google's AI surfaces, review [Google-Extended](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/overview-google-crawlers).

Blocking training-only crawlers is a legitimate choice. Blocking the search crawlers means you are invisible in that assistant's answers.

### 2. Is your answer extractable in 40 to 60 words?

AI systems pull passages, not whole pages. A visitor scrolling your site understands it. A model needs a self-contained block it can lift.

Put a direct answer right under each heading. Name the treatment, the city, and the outcome in one or two plain sentences. For example: "Botox in Scottsdale starts around $12 per unit, and consultations are free at [Clinic]." A model can quote that. "We offer a range of aesthetic solutions tailored to your journey" gives it nothing.

### 3. Do your facts match everywhere?

Models cross-check. If your name, address, phone, and service list disagree between your website, Google Business Profile, Yelp, and RealSelf, the model has less confidence in you and will prefer a clinic with consistent facts.

Fix the boring things first: one canonical business name, one phone number, one address format, and the same opening hours across every listing.

### 4. Do third-party sites confirm you?

AI answers lean on what others say about you. Reviews, local directories, press mentions, and provider profiles all act as confirmation. You cannot fake this, and you should not try. You can earn it by asking satisfied patients for reviews, keeping directory profiles complete, and being mentioned in local media.

### 5. Do you have specific treatment and city pages?

A page about "Botox in Scottsdale" answers a query. A page about "our services" does not. Build one strong page per core treatment and location combination, then stop. Fifty thin city-swapped pages are [scaled content abuse](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) under Google's spam policy and can damage the whole site.

### 6. Are you citing sources and using real numbers?

The [GEO research paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024) tested how content changes affect visibility in generative engine responses. It found optimization could lift visibility by up to 40%, and that the strongest tactics included citing sources and adding statistics. Keyword stuffing moved visibility in the wrong direction. The same paper is where the "keyword stuffing hurts" result comes from.

Apply it to your own pages: cite your sources, and replace vague claims with specific, dated numbers.

## A 30-day plan for a single-location clinic

**Week 1 — Fix access and facts**
1. Check `robots.txt` for AI crawler blocks and remove any that block search bots.
2. Audit your Google Business Profile, website, Yelp, and RealSelf for identical name, address, phone, and hours.
3. Add `MedicalBusiness` schema to your contact page. (See [Page 4](/ROO/issues/ROO-4), Med Spa Schema Markup.)

**Week 2 — Rewrite one page for extraction**
4. Pick your highest-revenue treatment in your city.
5. Rewrite the intro so the first 50 words answer the query directly.
6. Add an FAQ block with the five questions your front desk hears most.

**Week 3 — Build confirmation**
7. Ask your last 30 happy patients for a Google review, one request each.
8. Complete every field on your directory profiles.
9. Pitch one local publication or news outlet a short expert comment on a trend.

**Week 4 — Test and iterate**
10. Run the audit prompts below and record whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews name you.
11. Fix the single biggest omission you find.
12. Put a monthly reminder to repeat step 10.

## How to test whether ChatGPT cites you

Open ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google, and run these prompt patterns:

- "best med spa in [your city]"
- "[your treatment] in [your city]"
- "[your treatment] cost in [your city]"
- "med spa near [your neighborhood]"

Record the date, the platform, whether you appeared, and who did. Run the same list every month. That log is your visibility baseline, and it is the same method behind our free [AI visibility audit](/ROO/issues/ROO-4).

## Common mistakes that keep clinics invisible

- **Keyword stuffing.** It measurably reduces visibility in generative responses, per the GEO paper.
- **City-swap pages.** Fifty near-identical "Botox in [suburb]" pages read as spam. Build one strong local page instead.
- **Hidden pricing.** Models prefer pages with concrete numbers. Vague pricing gives them nothing to cite.
- **Ignoring the boring entity work.** Schema, GBP, and NAP consistency look unglamorous and do the heavy lifting.
- **Blocking AI crawlers by accident.** Check the file before blaming the algorithm.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to show up in ChatGPT?

Expect four to twelve weeks for a well-structured page on a site with no blocking issues. AI systems re-crawl on their own schedule, so speed depends on crawl frequency, your site's authority, and how competitive your city and treatment are.

### Can I pay to show up in ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT search citations are not for sale. The only lever is being reachable, clear, and confirmed by other trusted sources. Ads in assistant interfaces are still early and are not a reliable local-visibility channel today.

### Does general SEO help me get cited by AI?

Yes. Google states that its generative AI features are rooted in its core ranking and quality systems, so the fundamentals carry over. A page that ranks well and answers questions cleanly is also easier for assistants to quote.

### Do I need an llms.txt file?

It does not hurt, but Google explicitly says no special file is required for its AI features. For other assistants, a clean, well-linked site does more than a new file. Treat `llms.txt` as optional, not a priority over fixing crawler access and page structure.

### Will AI answers replace my website traffic?

Some clicks disappear, because the answer is on the results page. Citations and referrals still happen, and being named builds brand trust. The realistic goal is to be the clinic the answer names, not to preserve every old click.
