# Best AI Visibility Tools for Med Spas

The right AI visibility tool for a med spa does three jobs: it shows whether ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews mention your clinic, it reveals the prompts your patients actually type, and it tracks those answers over time. Enterprise platforms charge for all three plus features a single-location clinic will never use.

This page covers five tools we checked in October 2026, sorted by who each one fits. It ends with a free manual method if you would rather not pay yet.

## What a 1-to-3 location clinic actually needs

Before comparing features, decide what the tool is for. A med spa with a few locations needs:

1. **Mention monitoring.** Does the answer name you for "best med spa in [city]" and "[treatment] in [city]"?
2. **Prompt discovery.** Which long, conversational questions do people ask? The Andreessen Horowitz analysis of [GEO](https://a16z.com/geo-over-seo/) found AI-native queries average around 23 words, so you need the real phrasing, not a two-word keyword.
3. **Competitor benchmarking.** When you are absent, who is named instead? That is your actual rival for the AI answer, and it is often not the clinic ranking number one on Google.
4. **Change tracking.** One screenshot proves nothing. You need a dated record so you can tell whether last month's work moved the answer.

If a tool does not do those four things, its extra features are not for you.

## The five tools

### 1. Profound

**What it is:** An AI marketing platform that monitors what customers ask AI, then helps teams act on it. Its homepage lists ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, DeepSeek, and Google AI Overviews among the platforms it covers.

**Who it fits:** Multi-location groups and agencies with a person whose job is to act on the data. Profound positions itself toward larger organisations and advertises enterprise trust signals, so a single clinic is below its natural buyer.

**Watch out for:** Pricing is not published. You will need a sales call, which usually means an annual commitment. That is fine for a group, heavy for one location.

**Verdict:** Powerful, bigger than most independent clinics need.

### 2. Scrunch

**What it is:** Scrunch combines AI-search brand monitoring with website analysis and optimizing content for AI agents. Its site frames the shift plainly: AI, not only humans, now reads your website.

**Who it fits:** A clinic that wants one dashboard for both "are we mentioned?" and "is our site readable by AI?" The published startup tier, around $250 per month, is the most clinic-sized published price in this list.

**Watch out for:** It is still a monitoring and optimization tool. Someone on your side has to turn findings into new pages, schema, and profile work. The tool does not write your content strategy for you.

**Verdict:** The best fit for a single clinic that wants tracking plus site fixes at a published price.

### 3. Peec AI

**What it is:** AI search analytics for marketing teams. Peec tracks brand performance across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, benchmarks competitors, and produces reports. Its site emphasises dashboards and reporting over execution.

**Who it fits:** An owner or practice manager who wants a clear monthly view and does not need agentic workflows. The competitor benchmarking maps directly onto the "who is named instead of us?" question.

**Watch out for:** Platform coverage is narrower than Profound's or Ahrefs' listed coverage. Pricing is demo-led, so budget time for a call.

**Verdict:** Strong reporting and benchmarking for a lean team.

### 4. Semrush AI Visibility

**What it is:** Semrush's AI visibility product tracks AI mentions and surfaces opportunities within the Semrush platform. It sits alongside Semrush's established SEO and content tooling.

**Who it fits:** A clinic already paying for Semrush. Adding AI visibility to a tool you use for keywords and audits avoids a second subscription and a second login.

**Watch out for:** The AI features are tied to Semrush's plan structure, and Semrush's published pricing is aimed at agencies and in-house teams. If you are not using Semrush for core SEO, you are buying a lot of product to get one feature.

**Verdict:** The obvious pick if you are already a Semrush customer.

### 5. Ahrefs Brand Radar

**What it is:** Ahrefs' AI visibility product. Its page claims coverage across six AI tools, breadth from a large prompt dataset, and custom prompts. It also shows which offsite sources influence AI visibility.

**Who it fits:** An SEO-aware owner who already trusts Ahrefs data and wants to see how offsite mentions feed the answer. The offsite-source view is unusual and useful for a clinic working on reviews and directory presence.

**Watch out for:** Brand Radar is an add-on to an Ahrefs subscription, and Ahrefs plans run from around $29 to $1,499 per month. The total cost depends on the base plan you need.

**Verdict:** Best for owners already invested in the Ahrefs ecosystem.

## What you do not need

- **Agentic automation.** Tools now promise to deploy agents that act on data. A clinic with no marketing team will not use this, and it inflates the price.
- **Hundreds of dashboards.** Three views matter: your visibility, the prompts, and your competitors.
- **Global or multi-language coverage.** Unless you treat patients from outside your metro, this is noise.
- **A tool before a plan.** Software tells you where you are absent. It does not build the treatment pages and schema that fix it. Do the free audit first, then buy a tool to track progress.

## The free method (run this today)

You do not need software to get a baseline. Open ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews and run these prompts for your clinic:

- "best med spa in [city]"
- "who does [your top treatment] in [city]?"
- "how much does [treatment] cost in [city]?"
- "med spa near [neighborhood]"

For each, record the date, whether you were named, and who was. Repeat monthly in a spreadsheet. This is exactly the method behind our free [AI visibility audit](/ROO/issues/ROO-4), and for one clinic it is often enough for the first few months.

## How to choose

1. **Write down your four jobs** (mentions, prompts, competitors, tracking).
2. **Check published pricing first.** A vendor that will not post a range is asking you to enter a negotiation, so know that going in.
3. **Prefer the tool that fits your workflow.** If you already use Semrush or Ahrefs, the add-on is cheaper than a new platform.
4. **Run a 30-day trial with fixed prompts** so you can compare tools on the same list.
5. **Budget for execution, not just software.** A $250-per-month tool that nobody acts on is $3,000 a year wasted.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a paid AI visibility tool as a single med spa?

Not at first. A monthly manual check of five to ten prompts gives most single-location clinics a usable baseline. Buy a tool once the manual check becomes a chore or you need competitor benchmarking across many prompts.

### What is the cheapest AI visibility tool?

Scrunch publishes a startup tier around $250 per month, the lowest published entry price in our October 2026 check. Ahrefs can be cheaper if you need only a small base plan, and the manual method costs nothing.

### Can these tools guarantee I appear in ChatGPT?

No. They measure and monitor. Getting cited still depends on crawl access, extractable answers, consistent entity data, and third-party confirmation, which is covered in our [ChatGPT visibility guide](/ROO/issues/ROO-4).

### Do AI visibility tools replace SEO tools?

No. Google says its AI features are rooted in core Search systems, so traditional SEO data still matters. Most teams run one platform that now covers both rather than two separate subscriptions.

### Which tool is best for a med spa with multiple locations?

Profound or a Semrush/Ahrefs add-on at a higher plan tier. Multi-location tracking multiplies prompts and competitors, which is where the enterprise tools earn their price.
